## 02. ABOUT WYZLAB STUDIO ORIGINALS

### Sub-Brand Overview

**WyzLab Studio Originals** is the **creative publishing and content studio** of WyzLab. It turns WyzLab's instructional design expertise and frameworks into concrete learning products that people can read, use, apply, and be held accountable to.

It produces:

- **Transformation products:** Paid challenges, coaching programs, sprints — delivered via WyzCore with mobile push notifications.
- **Digital content:** Ebooks, workbooks, playbooks, templates, certification prep guides, AI tools playbooks.
- **Physical content:** Kids' books, printed playbooks, worktexts, kits.
- **Mobile apps:** Focused learning tools for TESDA prep, values education, career readiness, freelancing, and AI skills.
- **AI-enhanced products:** Agent-powered coaching, accountability tools, and personalized study paths.

### The Format Shift (2026 Update)

Studio Originals has historically produced information products (PDFs, workbooks, static content). Market data from 2026 confirms:

- Self-paced PDFs average **5–15% completion rates**
- Transformation products (paid challenges, coaching programs with accountability) achieve **70–80% completion rates**
- Products that deliver a result in 5–21 days outperform traditional courses for both conversion and learner satisfaction

**Standard going forward:** Every Sprint, Challenge, or multi-day program must be delivered as a **WyzCore Coaching Program with daily push notifications**. Static PDFs are reserved for reference materials, templates, and supplementary assets only.

### Role in the WyzLab Ecosystem

Studio Originals is the **IP engine and learning path builder** for WyzLab:

- Provides **ready-made content** that runs on **WyzCore, WyzCore Academy, WyzGuild, WyzQuests, and WyzPages**
- Creates **entry products** for beginners and consumers that lead into higher-ticket offers and platforms
- Aligns content around **clear learning paths** with transformation at the center, not information delivery
- Functions as the **primary funnel for WyzPro** — the 21-Day Sprint and 5-Day Challenge are the strongest WyzPro intent signals in the product lineup

## 06. BRAND PERSONALITY & VOICE

### Personality Blend

- **60% Creative Studio** — Crafted, story-rich, visually considered, "collector-worthy" products
- **40% Accountable Coach** — Structured, scaffolded, present daily, obsessed with completion and outcomes

*Note: The secondary personality has shifted from "Learning Architect" (v1.0) to "Accountable Coach" to reflect the transformation product standard.*

### Voice & Style

- **Tone:** Warm, encouraging, grounded — and direct about what needs to be done today
- **Language:** Professional English with Filipino context; no Taglish, but deeply Filipino in stories and examples
- **Sentence Length:** 10–16 words on average; accessible to K–12 and adult learners alike
- **Daily task language:** Short, action-verb-first. "Today: Write your course promise." Not "In this module, we will explore..."

Patterns:

- Lead with **what the learner will do today**, not what they will learn
- Always connect an assignment back to the overall path ("By completing this, you've finished Week 1.")
- Use real Filipino scenarios — families, workplaces, schools, communities — without caricature
- In push notifications: clear, friendly, specific. "Day 4: Map your Foundation module. 30 mins. Tap to start."

## 10. TRANSFORMATION PRODUCT DESIGN STANDARDS

All Sprint, Challenge, and multi-day Studio Originals must meet these standards:

### 10.1 Delivery Requirements

- **Platform:** WyzCore Coaching Program (not standalone PDF)
- **Notifications:** Daily push notification to WyzCore mobile app (8:00 AM Philippine time)
- **Day structure:** Task card + estimated time (30–60 mins) + assignment submission
- **Optional:** Messenger/Viber daily prompt for mobile-only participants
- **Milestone check-ins:** Live or recorded video from facilitator at key milestones

### 10.2 Completion Design

The following must be engineered into every transformation product:

- [ ] **Day 1 win in under 30 minutes** — first task delivers one immediately usable output
- [ ] **Daily progress visibility** — completion percentage visible in app at all times
- [ ] **Missed day recovery** — Sprint Coach agent sends personalized nudge if no submission by 9 PM
- [ ] **Milestone acknowledgment** — Gold badge + message at 25%, 50%, 75%, 100%
- [ ] **Strong end** — Day 21 / Day 5 ends with specific "what's next" step into the ecosystem

### 10.3 Content Standards

- Each day's task must produce a **tangible output** (a written course promise, a completed module map, a drafted sales post)
- Tasks must be completable in **30–60 minutes** on a normal workday
- Language is **action-verb-first**: "Write...", "Map...", "Record...", not "Understand..." or "Explore..."
- The FTM framework governs the overall sprint arc: Foundation (Days 1–7) → Transformation (Days 8–14) → Mastery (Days 15–21) for the 21-Day Sprint

## 11. 21-DAY SPRINT — REVISED DELIVERY ARCHITECTURE

### Overview

The 21-Day Sprint has been redesigned from a standalone PDF workbook into a **WyzCore Coaching Program with mobile push notifications**. This change lifts expected completion from ~15% to 70–80% by building accountability into the delivery mechanism itself.

### Delivery Stack

- **Platform:** WyzCore Coaching Program module
- **Daily delivery:** Push notification to WyzCore mobile app at 8:00 AM Philippine time
- **Content per day:** Task card with FTM context, 30–60 min estimated time, assignment submission form
- **Optional:** Messenger/Viber daily prompt for participants who prefer messaging-only
- **Milestone check-ins:** Days 7, 14, and 21 include a short live Zoom or recorded video from Yza
- **Day 21:** Celebration + WyzCore Flex onboarding + WyzPro offer presentation

### Sprint Arc

| Days | Phase | Focus | Output |
|------|-------|-------|--------|
| Days 1–7 | Foundation | Offer clarity, learner profile, course promise | Defined course promise + target learner profile |
| Days 8–14 | Transformation | Module mapping (FTM), lesson design, content creation | Complete FTM module map + 3 sample lessons |
| Days 15–21 | Mastery | Sales page, pricing, launch calendar, first enrollment | Live course page + first enrollment attempt |

### Notification Schedule

- **8:00 AM** — Day task push notification: "Day [X]: [Task Title]. Tap to begin."
- **7:00 PM** — Evening check-in: "Did you complete today's task? Tap to submit."
- **Day 7, 14, 21** — Milestone: "You've completed Week [X]! Here's your check-in from Yza."
- **Missed day** — Sprint Coach AI agent sends personalized nudge via Messenger/app

## 13. LEARNING DESIGN & BINGEABLE FRAMEWORK

*(4D + FTM + 5E — applied to all products)*

### 13.1 Baseline Standards

Every Studio Original must:

1. **Define a clear learning path** — starting point, milestones, end state
2. Use **structured educational communication** — chunking, scaffolding, clear instructions
3. Embed **practice, reflection, and daily application** opportunities
4. Build in an **accountability mechanism** — notifications, submissions, or check-ins
5. Provide a **"What's next?"** into the broader WyzLab ecosystem

### 13.2 When to Use Heavily vs Lightly

**Heavily (4D + FTM + 5E + accountability layer):**
- All Sprint and Challenge products
- Coach-facing Originals (kits, sprints, templates)
- B2B content packs and TESDA modules
- Apps where performance and progression are tracked

**Lightly (principles, not explicit labels):**
- Kids' and youth storybooks
- Inspirational or narrative-led guides
- AI Tools Playbook (framework-informed, not framework-labeled)

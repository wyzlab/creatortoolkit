Checkpoint 20. Trust, Pricing, and Sales
A B C D E F G H
20a Sulit, confident pricing
Why: Answers the very Filipino okay, pero magkano ba.
Good: A price you can defend.
20b Honest proof, no budol
Why: This separates you from the guru crowd.
Good: Real proof shown plainly, zero guaranteed results.
20c Outline reused as sales copy
Why: A clear outline sells itself.

20d Local checkout enabled
Why: Removes the last friction before payment.

STEP 1 Mine your top content
Content-to-Course Idea Sparker
01
Your best-performing and most-loved content is proof of what people already 
want from you. Start there, not with what you wish you were known for.
What counts as content
• Posts and captions
• Reels and videos
• Livestreams
• Lesson decks and handouts
• The questions people ask you again and again
The questions people ask you are gold. Those questions are your future lessons, already 
validated by real humans.

STEP 3 Shape one course outline
Content-to-Course Idea Sparker
03
A course is one transformation, broken into steps. Each module is one step. Your 
job now is simple: name the transformation, then break it into 3 to 5 modules.
One problem. One solution. One clear result.
result
If a module does not move your student one clear step forward, cut it. Three strong modules 
beat six vague ones.
www.wyzcore.com

STEP 3 Name your transformation
Content-to-Course Idea Sparker
Finish this sentence. Be specific. This is the promise of your whole course.
After this course, my student can:
Type the one specific thing your students can do...
Example: After this course, my student can plan and post one week of Reels for a Manila milk tea shop in one sitting.
If you can picture your student doing this, your course has a clear finish line.

STEP 4 Quick validate
Content-to-Course Idea Sparker
04

I can talk about it for hours.
I can name a clear result for my student.

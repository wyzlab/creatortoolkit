How do you spot a scam, and avoid 
getting ghosted?
Red flags
• Asks for upfront or membership fees before 
you can earn.
• Promises rates too good to be true.
• Requests personal info it has no reason to 
need.
Safe move: get paid upfront
Selling a product means you are paid before you 
deliver, so you sidestep ghosting and unpaid work. 
A clean, named checkout makes you look legit, 
too, because the same red flags you avoid are the 
ones your buyers check for.
